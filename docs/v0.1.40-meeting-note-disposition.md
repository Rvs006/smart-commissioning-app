## Manual stop scope

Manual Stop is per-run. The persisted `cancel_requested`/ownership checks use the run ID. `backend/tests/test_inline_heartbeat_integration.py::RealInlineHeartbeatIntegrationTests::test_stopping_one_persisted_run_does_not_cancel_a_second_run` creates and dispatches two persisted runs through `RunService`, cancels and finalises the first, then proves the second retains `cancel_requested: false` and completes independently. `test_stop_request_keeps_heartbeat_until_cancelled_finalization` additionally proves the stopped run keeps its heartbeat until its cancellation finalises. The five-to-eight-minute incident was capture termination/retention behavior, not shared cancellation state.
